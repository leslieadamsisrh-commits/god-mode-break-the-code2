# STUDIO DEPARTMENTS

## 00 — Studio Director / Command Center
Owns priorities, gates, locks, cross-department conflicts, release sequence, and the single current mission.

## 01 — Combat & Movement
Movement, acceleration/braking, slide, cover, aim, muzzle origin, weapons, powers, damage, recoil, hit reactions, knockback.

## 02 — Animation & Sprites
Jade master design, pose anchors, directional matrices, sprite completion, socket registration, weapon alignment, animation QA.

## 03 — Characters & Enemies
Gorilla Milk, Adamus Prime, Ruby, Baron, Varkon, Street Talons, raptors, NPCs, combat identity.

## 04 — Level & World Design
Training/Combat Lab, Dimension 33, Bay Bridge, Laurel Creek benchmark work, traversal, cover layout, collision/readability.

## 05 — Engineering & Multiplayer
Runtime architecture, controller input, networking, prediction, synchronization, performance, deployment, recovery.

## 06 — Story / Lore / World Bible
1989 event, family/corporate plot, War Games, dimensions, character arcs, chronology, canon.

## 07 — UI / HUD / VFX / Audio
Helmet HUD, menus, feedback, particles, cyber-fluid, presentation, sound/music direction.

## 08 — QA & Playtesting
Bug reproduction, severity, regression matrix, controller tests, build verification, approved-feel preservation.

## 09 — Idea Vault
Unscheduled mechanics, maps, characters, vehicles, effects, modes, marketing ideas. Captured without interrupting the active gate.

## 10 — Steam / Business / IP / Release
Store page, packaging, pricing, credits/licenses, copyright/trademark workflow, release readiness, marketing.
