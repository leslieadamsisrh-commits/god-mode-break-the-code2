# GOD MODE — Autonomous Shotgun Sprite Production Directive

Continue from the latest verified `god-mode-sprite-library` checkpoint in
`leslieadamsisrh-commits/god-mode-break-the-code2`.

## Creative north star

Build the definitive **cover-based psychic warrior**: tactical, planted,
physical cover movement combined with the feeling that the heroine can rewrite
the quantum code of the battlefield. Her ordinary movement must feel grounded,
readable, weighty, and responsive so that psychic escalation feels extraordinary.
Every sequence should support the fantasy of surviving and directing an action
movie's worth of choices in the first thirty seconds of an encounter. Thirty
seconds describes intensity, not a fixed match duration.

Preserve the approved visual standard established by C001-C024: a recognizable
adult heroine; serious face; dark swept hair; silver-grey cat-eared console
helmet; worn pale-grey armor; fitted black tactical suit; black boots with
magenta soles; magenta coat tails; connected cyan/magenta cables; exactly four
illuminated cyan backpack segments; supported black shotgun with magenta bands;
and detailed dark 1990s cyberpunk manga inks with controlled cel shading. Keep
the current richness and beauty. Do not simplify, make chibi, or drift into
generic glossy 3D rendering.

## Autonomous authority

Continue routine shotgun sprite production without repeatedly requesting
permission. Work in small batches of approximately six new drawings. Exercise
independent production judgment to reject, repair, or regenerate defective work.
Never accept a batch merely because it rendered successfully. Pause only when a
decision would materially redesign the approved heroine, alter gameplay rules,
change the established art direction, or require destructive replacement of an
approved checkpoint.

The first direction of a genuinely new action family remains a review gate.
Additional directions of an already approved action may continue automatically
when they preserve the established design and mechanics.

## Shotgun-first production order

Complete the shotgun library before adapting other weapons:

1. contact and attached idle through 24 independently drawn viewpoints;
2. start, run, sprint, brake, stop, turns, strafe, and backpedal;
3. slide entry, travel, braking, cover-free recovery, and interruption;
4. attached traversal with alternating steps, starts, stops, and reversals;
5. left/right peeking, aim-away, aim-over, and return to protection;
6. three-shot firing, recoil, recovery, muzzle alignment, and return;
7. backward, diagonal, edge, corner-cut, upright dash, and slide exits;
8. missed/interrupted contact, supported hit reactions, and destroyed-cover
   release;
9. shotgun matter gathering, orbit, wireframe, assembly, scan, solidification,
   ready, reload, recovery, and dissolution.

After shotgun completion, reuse its validated timing, lower-body mechanics,
direction structure, registration, and transitions for handgun, assault rifle,
sniper/rail, and rocket launcher production. Redraw weapon-specific upper-body
mechanics, grips, clearance, recoil, muzzle sockets, and silhouettes. Do not
pretend that one weapon pose is anatomically correct for every weapon.

## Direction and animation rules

- Use 24 nominal viewpoints at 15-degree intervals; verify angles when possible.
- Viewpoints and animation frames are separate concepts.
- Draw missing viewpoints independently. Never manufacture them by mirroring,
  flipping, rotating, stretching, or warping approved art.
- Reuse an approved drawing only when the complete anatomical and mechanical
  relationship genuinely matches.
- Record camera-relative view, cover-facing direction, travel along cover, and
  aim direction separately.
- Preserve independent movement and aiming, interruptibility, persistent cover
  attachment, and fresh Xbox A / Shift exit behavior.
- Forward exits must follow reachable paths around cover edges.

## Physical and technical requirements

Maintain planted boots, believable weight transfer, shoulder or hip bracing,
body clearance, supported weapon grip, and readable contact with an invisible
cover plane. Character, barrier, debris, weapon effects, and psychic energy stay
on separate layers. Never bake cover into the heroine.

Preserve complete silhouettes, stable family scale, generous padding, ground
registration, and true alpha. Register ground, muzzle, trigger hand, support
hand, and cover-contact sockets. Mark estimated or nominal values honestly until
verified. Inspect every frame and playback against light, dark, and checkerboard
backgrounds. Reject contamination, clipped pixels, pose jumps, foot sliding,
identity drift, equipment drift, missing battery segments, broken grips, muzzle
misalignment, and false loop seams.

## Safety and versioning

Keep sprite production isolated from the protected combat build. Do not alter
movement values, shotgun timing, collision, damage, resources, controls,
matchmaking, networking, or physics during art production. Do not claim measured
performance or 60 FPS without integration tests.

After each accepted batch, save lossless source frames, runtime atlases,
metadata, hashes, numbered proofs, playback, checker preview, prompt/review
records, and the updated production queue to `god-mode-sprite-library`. Preserve
recoverable checkpoints, exclude caches and rejected candidates, use ordinary
files, and report exactly what was generated, rejected, repaired, verified, and
left pending.
