# Cover contact d05 production record

C025-C030 continue the approved cover-contact action at nominal d05 / 75 degrees.
The d03/d04 art established identity, detail, pose quality, and animation beats;
the recovered legacy d05 art was used only as a rough angle cue because it
contains contamination and mixed actions.

Six independently drawn frames cover slide braking, boot plant, shoulder/hip
contact, impact absorption, settling, and attached stance. The heroine retains
her detailed adult face, cat-eared helmet, worn armor, supported shotgun,
magenta coat tails, connected cables, and four cyan battery segments. No frame
was mirrored, rotated, stretched, or mechanically transformed from another view.

Lossless source cells, a true-alpha runtime atlas, numbered proofs, playback,
checker preview, hashes, ground registration, and estimated socket records are
preserved. The camera angle remains nominal until verified in engine. This batch
is not gameplay-integrated and changes no combat, timing, movement, collision,
resource, control, networking, physics, or performance values.
